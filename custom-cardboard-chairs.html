What makes branded cardboard event chairs different?

Traditional event seating fills a functional need. Custom printed cardboard chairs also provide visible space for sponsor logos, campaign artwork, team colors or product messaging. Guests encounter the branding while using the seating, rather than only on a nearby banner.

Box Chairs are made using recycled cardboard and can be customized with full-color graphics. The best design depends on how the seats will be used, the venue, the audience and the desired finish. Box Chairs designs have been weight-tested to support up to 485 pounds; ask about the specifications of the version proposed for your project.

For marketers, the starting point is not just the chair. It is the experience: where the seating will be placed, what the audience will see, and how the visual concept supports the broader activation.

Practical considerations for event organizers.

Outdoor use and weather

Cardboard products are sensitive to moisture. If your activation may face rain, damp ground or prolonged outdoor exposure, discuss the expected conditions and available weather-resistance choices when requesting a quote. Do not assume any standard cardboard chair is waterproof.

Artwork and sponsor visibility

Plan your graphics for how people will actually see the chair: from walkways, seating areas and nearby photo opportunities. Product names, sponsor logos and event colors should remain readable at the anticipated viewing distance. The printable areas and final artwork proofs are part of the project discussion.

Production and event logistics

Custom orders are quoted individually because chair quantity, print specifications, finishing, production schedule and destination all affect the plan. Share your deadline early and allow time for proofing and any shipping arrangements.
