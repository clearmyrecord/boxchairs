Frequently asked questions

Custom cardboard chairs: what to know.

Details that help event planners, agencies and sponsors prepare a useful quote request.

Can I print my logo and custom artwork on the chairs?

Yes. Box Chairs are designed for custom, full-color graphics. Share your logo, campaign artwork or design brief when requesting pricing so the printing approach can be discussed.

Are Box Chairs made from recycled materials?

Box Chairs use recycled cardboard. The exact construction and finish can be discussed for your event, quantities and artwork needs.

How much weight can a cardboard chair support?

Box Chairs designs have been weight-tested up to 485 pounds. Confirm the specifications of the version quoted for your event before ordering.

Can the chairs be used at outdoor events?

Outdoor use depends on weather, ground conditions and the selected construction or finish. Because cardboard can be affected by moisture, discuss rain exposure and weather-resistance requirements before placing an order.

How much do custom cardboard chairs cost?

Pricing is quoted for each project based on quantity, artwork, specifications, schedule and delivery location. Request a custom quote with your event details.

How do I order chairs for a trade show or promotion?

Send your event date and location, estimated quantity, logo or design idea, and delivery deadline. We can review the requirements and provide a tailored quote.
